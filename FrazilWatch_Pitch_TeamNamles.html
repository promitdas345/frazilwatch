The build — a multi-agent swarm

Three specialist agents. One supervisor. A real fight to resolve.

The Conflict

Two truths collide

✕ deadlock: "send more power" is physically impossible
Supervisor Agent · The Grid Orchestrator

It rejects the obvious answer — and acts.

The supervisor weighs both constraints, refuses to push more power through a maxed line, and instead issues a targeted industrial load-shed, requests Maritime Link imports, and fires a public conservation alert — hours before the plant chokes. Deterministic IF/THEN logic, not a guess.
